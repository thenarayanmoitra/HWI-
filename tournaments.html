03 — How It Works

Simple. Exciting.
Global.

/ 01

Register

Fill out the registration form for the current tournament. Coaches and dojang masters can register their entire roster at once through the partnership program. Full rules, fees, and submission requirements are confirmed in your registration email.

/ 02

Record and Submit

Film performances at your dojang or at home. Single uncut take, landscape orientation, full athlete in frame. State your name, country, belt, and chosen form on camera before beginning. Submit via unlisted YouTube link or shared Google Drive URL by the deadline.

/ 03

Watch, Celebrate and Receive

Results are streamed live on YouTube via Fast Taekwondo. Medals, official certificates, and global ranking points issued to all participants. Team trophies for Gold-tier partner dojangs and above, shipped directly to your school.
