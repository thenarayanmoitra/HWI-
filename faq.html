100% of registration fees go to charity. Food aid, school supplies, medical assistance, disaster relief, and the Bike Ambulance service in rural India. We document every project across our social media — full transparency is part of the model.
Individual Poomsae, Pairs Poomsae, Team Poomsae, Speed Kicking, Freestyle, Kyukpa (breaking), and Para-Poomsae. Categories vary slightly by tournament — the full list arrives with your tournament outline after registration.
Certified, experienced Taekwondo masters from around the world, under the direct guidance of Grand Master Han Wong (9th Dan, Kukkiwon). Our nine brand ambassadors include Grand Masters from Egypt, Italy, the United States, Australia, Malaysia, the UAE, Croatia, and more.
Every participant receives a high-quality medal shipped directly to your address (or your dojang's address). Partner dojangs in the Gold tier and above also receive a physical Team Trophy. Plus you get the livestream coverage, certificate, and an entry in our global team rankings.

Record in a single uncut take, landscape orientation, with the full athlete in frame including the opening bow. State your name, country, belt, and chosen form on camera before beginning. Upload via unlisted YouTube link or shared Google Drive URL through the form.
Three per year — Scotland International Open in March, Han Wong International Open in July, and Edinburgh International Open in November. Registration typically opens 6-8 weeks before each tournament closes.
HWI is operated by Han Wong International Academy, Scotland, founded by Grand Master Han Wong — a 9th Dan from five organisations including Kukkiwon. We are an independent online charity tournament series, not an official federation, but our judging council includes certified masters from world-recognised organisations.
