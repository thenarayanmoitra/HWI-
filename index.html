Our founder

Grand Master Han Wong.

The only Taekwondo Grand Master in the world to hold a 9th Dan black belt from five organisations.

Originally from Malaysia, Grand Master Han has given more than 50 years to Taekwondo. He came to the UK to study, made Edinburgh his home, and became a pioneer of WTF-style Taekwondo in Scotland. He earned his first black belt under the legendary Grandmaster Rhee Ki-ha and received awards from the late General Choi Hong Hi.

He has overcome cancer and still teaches and judges at the highest level. He founded Jidokwan and Changmookwan schools in India and the Philippines.

    Questions

    Good to know.

    Still unsure? Message us on WhatsApp or email and a real person will reply within 48 hours.

    How do I register?

    Press any “Register now” button. It opens our official Google Form for the current championship. Fill it in, add your video link, and you’re entered. We confirm by email within 48 hours.

    Do I need to travel anywhere?

    No. Everything is online. You record your performance at your dojang or at home, and we judge it from the video. There are no travel, visa or hotel costs.

    Where does my entry fee go?

    100% of registration fees go to charity: food, school supplies, medical help, disaster relief and Karimul Haque’s Bike Ambulance and hospital. Every project is published on our Facebook page. Shipping costs for medals are charged separately.

    Who judges the entries?

    Certified, experienced referees from around the world, under the direct guidance of Grand Master Han Wong, 9th Dan.

    How do I record and send my video?

    Film in one uncut take, with the phone held sideways and the whole athlete in view, including the opening bow. Say your name, country, belt and form before you begin. Upload it as an unlisted YouTube video or a Google Drive link and paste the link into the form. The full rules are in the tournament guidelines, which you can download in the next championship section.

    Can my whole dojang enter together?

    Yes. With 5 or more students you join the Dojang Partnership Programme and save 5% to 40%, with one invoice and one delivery for all medals. Message us on WhatsApp on +91 62942 78034 to start. See the programme.

    Is Han Wong International an official federation?

    No. We are an independent online charity championship run by Han Wong International Academy in Edinburgh, Scotland. Our judges include certified masters from world-recognised organisations.
