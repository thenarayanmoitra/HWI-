01 — The Mission

Professional competition.
Charitable heart.

/ 01
Every Dollar Goes to Charity
100% of registration fees fund food, education, medical aid, and disaster relief — distributed transparently and documented across our social media.
/ 02
World-Class Judging
Events scored by WT-certified referees from across the globe. Under the direct guidance of Grand Master Han Wong, 9th Dan from five organisations including Kukkiwon.
/ 03
Compete from Anywhere
Film your performance at your dojang or at home, on your schedule. Upload. Get judged. No visas, no travel costs, no barriers.
/ 04
Medals + Trophy to Your Door
Every participant receives a high-quality medal. Official certificates and global ranking points included. Shipped directly to your dojang.
