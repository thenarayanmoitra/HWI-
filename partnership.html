Unlocking value
for your dojang.

/ 01
Up to 40% Discount
Tiered savings based on your team size — from 5% for 5 entries up to 40% off for 50+ entries. Pass the savings to your students or retain them as dojang revenue. Applied automatically to your invoice, no codes needed.

Gold tier and above (20+ students) receive a physical Team Trophy shipped with your medals — perfect for awards ceremonies and display at your dojang.

All your team's medals dispatched in a single shipment to your dojang. Priority queue placement. Reduces individual athlete shipping cost by up to 70%.

Global Visibility
Your dojang logo featured on the Official Partners page at hanwonginternational.org. Digital "Official Partner" badge for your website and social media channels.
