Why partner with us

Real value for your dojang.

Your students compete internationally without leaving home, every fee helps families in need, and your school gains a name on the world stage.

Up to 40% off

Tiered savings from 5% for 5 entries up to 40% for 50 or more. Keep it as dojang revenue or pass it on to your students.

A team trophy

Gold tier and above (20+ students) receive a physical team trophy with the medals, ready for your awards evening.

One delivery

All your team’s medals arrive in a single priority shipment to your dojang, cutting shipping cost per athlete by up to 70%.

Global visibility

Your dojang is listed as an Official Partner on our website, with a digital partner badge for your own site and social media.

Discount tiers

The more students, the bigger the saving.

Discounts apply to the base entry fee for your whole team. Shipping for medals and trophies stays at the standard rate. No codes and no portals: we work out your invoice and send it to you.
