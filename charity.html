02 — Featured Mission

Karimul Haque
and the Bike Ambulance.

Honorary Ambassador

5,000+ lives. One motorcycle. One man.

Karimul Haque has saved more than 5,000 lives in rural Bengal by transforming his motorcycle into a free ambulance service for villagers who otherwise cannot reach medical care in time.

His work has been featured by The Great Big Story, TEDx Talks, Al Jazeera English, and over a dozen international publications.

HWI is currently supporting Karimul's mission to build the first hospital in his village — a long-term project his community has been waiting decades for. Every fee at every tournament contributes.

03 — Where the Money Goes

Transparency on every dollar.

/ 01

Food and Essentials

Rice, pulses, oil, clothing, and household basics distributed to families affected by floods, displacement, and economic hardship. The largest single category of HWI charity spending.

/ 02

Education Support

School supplies, books, uniforms, and tuition support for children whose families cannot otherwise afford schooling. Including Disha, whose education HWI has directly sponsored.

/ 03

Medical and Mobility

Wheelchairs, tricycles, blood drives, and the sanitary pad bank — addressing daily dignity and emergency healthcare access across underserved communities.

/ 04

Tournament Accessibility

We waive fees for athletes from underprivileged nations who otherwise could not afford to participate — making the championship truly global, not just nominally.
