The inductees

Lifetimes on the mat.

Legends whose contribution to Taekwondo, through competition, coaching, humanitarian work and leadership, has left a lasting mark.

Achievers Award

For practitioners who may not hold the highest belts or longest records, but who have made a real, lasting difference in their communities through Taekwondo.

Brand ambassadors

Grand Masters on five continents.

Senior Grand Masters and coaches who put their name behind our championships and our charity work.

Honorary ambassador · India

Karimul Haque, a social hero.

Through his Bike Ambulance, Karimul has helped more than 5,000 people reach medical care. He has been featured by Great Big Story, TEDx and Al Jazeera English, and is now building a hospital in his village with our support.

“We are proud to have him with us, through the years and for many more to come.”

How we choose

What we look for.

No application fee. No politics. Only merit. Grand Master Han Wong and the HWI committee review every nomination once a year.

01

Lifetime dedication

Decades of practice, coaching or development that has shaped the sport, not one great season.

02

Community impact

Serving students and underserved communities through charity, grassroots work and mentoring young people.

03

Character and values

Courtesy, integrity, perseverance, self-control and indomitable spirit, in life as well as on the mat.
