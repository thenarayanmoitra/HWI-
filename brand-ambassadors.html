Brand
Ambassadors.

The brand heroes of the Taekwondo community who vouch for HWI's work. Grand Masters and senior coaches from across the world who have stood with the mission.

01 — Endorsing Masters

Nine voices.
Five continents.

🇪🇬
🇪🇬
GM Mohamed Riad
Egypt · Senior Grand Master · International Poomsae Referee
One of Africa's most respected Taekwondo figures. An international poomsae referee who has stood with HWI since its first edition, lending credibility and consistency to our judging standards across every tournament.
🇮🇹
🇮🇹
GM Cosimo Spinelli
Italy · Senior Grand Master · 9th Dan
51 years on the mat. 100 podium finishes. A World Master Championship medallist and coach who guided a deaf athlete to a world title. GM Spinelli is the gold standard of European Taekwondo and one of HWI's most vocal champions.
🇺🇸
🇺🇸
GM Jim Harp
United States · Senior Grand Master · Poomsae Specialist
A US Nationals and US Open gold medallist with 45+ years on the mat — still teaching, still competing, still leading.
🇺🇸
🇺🇸
GM Erica Linthorst
United States · Senior Grand Master · Poomsae
A force for accessible global competition, GM Linthorst brings elite technical standards and a deep commitment to making international Taekwondo available to athletes regardless of their background or location.
🇦🇺
🇦🇺
GM Russ Wood
Australia · Senior Grand Master · 9th Dan
"After many online competitions over two years, HWI has proven to be the best and easiest we have dealt with." At 84, with 510+ medals and still competing, GM Wood embodies the never-finish spirit that HWI stands for.
🇺🇸
🇺🇸
GM Julie Tregeagle
United States · Senior Grand Master · Senior Coach
A Poomsae World Champion whose legacy extends far beyond the podium. GM Tregeagle uses her platform to drive humanitarian outreach and education in underserved communities — values that sit at the very heart of HWI.
🇲🇾
🇲🇾
GM Sabree Salleh
Malaysia · Senior Grand Master · 9th Dan
A pillar of Malaysian Taekwondo and a key bridge between the Asian and European martial arts communities. GM Salleh's endorsement has been instrumental in growing HWI's reach across Southeast Asia.
🇦🇪
🇦🇪
GM Na Zih
United Arab Emirates · Senior Grand Master
The leading Taekwondo authority across the Middle East and a long-standing HWI ambassador. GM Na Zih has helped expand HWI's presence across the Gulf region and continues to champion the championship's charitable mission.
🇭🇷
🇭🇷
GM Svetlana Kana Gvozdić
Croatia · Senior Grand Master · Senior Judge
One of Europe's most respected international judges and a consistent voice for technical excellence in Taekwondo. GM Gvozdić brings rigour, fairness, and a lifetime of competitive experience to everything HWI does.
02 — Beyond Taekwondo

Karimul Haque
— A social hero.

Honorary Ambassador · India

The Bike Ambulance.

Karimul Haque has helped over 5,000 people in need by providing critical medical assistance through his Bike Ambulance — a one-man rural emergency service that has become a global story.

He has been featured by The Great Big Story, TEDx Talks, Al Jazeera English, and many more. Currently, he is building a hospital in his village, and HWI is supporting that mission directly.

"We are proud to have him with us — through the years and for many more to come."
